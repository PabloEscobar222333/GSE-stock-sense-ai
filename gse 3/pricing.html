Frequently Asked Questions

What does the free tier include? ▾
The free tier gives you 5 stock analyses — covering manual lookups, AI chat analyses, and radar scanner runs. No payment required, no card needed to sign up.
What counts as an "analysis"? ▾
Every time you run a stock signal report, ask the AI chat for analysis, or run a radar scanner session, it counts as one analysis. Browsing the dashboard, watchlist, and news does not consume analyses.
Can I cancel my subscription? ▾
Yes. You can cancel at any time. Your premium access remains active until the end of your billing period.
Is this financial advice? ▾
No. StockSense AI provides educational and informational analysis only. All signals and allocation advice should be considered alongside your own research and, where appropriate, professional financial advice.
